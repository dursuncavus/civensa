# CIV-033 — Activity Report audit against the 2021 regulation

Audit date: **17 October 2026 (Europe/Istanbul)**. Audited base: `feature/authorization-workflow-phase5`, commit **`2d325863638936a17c24e6830c65d49feb4651c7`**. Delivery branch: `task/civ-033-ar-2021-audit`.

**Finding: Civensa has a publication-bound indicator realisation workflow, but cannot currently produce a complete birim or idare faaliyet raporu under the supplied 2021 requirements.** Indicator targets, sourced actuals, numerical differences and immutable history exist. Most statutory narrative and financial content, strategic-plan evaluation, unit-report delivery, assurance annexes and the official output remain missing from the operational AR path. PP exports and inactive workflow services do not close those gaps.

## Scope and evidence

Decision **D-58** sets the legal baseline: *Kamu İdarelerince Hazırlanacak Stratejik Planlar ve Performans Programları ile Faaliyet Raporlarına İlişkin Usul ve Esaslar Hakkında Yönetmelik*, RG **22.04.2021**, amended RG **05.03.2025/32832**. This audit uses the lead-verified article 26 content, article 27 format/assurance requirements and articles 24–25 deadlines supplied in CIV-033. It does not independently certify the consolidated legal text or reconstruct annex wording from the 2006 regulation.

Findings below come from current source, models, route registration, templates, exporters and existing test assertions. [CIV-014 / PR #24](https://github.com/dursuncavus/civensa/pull/24) is used only for the requested historical comparison, not as evidence that a feature works today. The repository contains no CIV-029 report or assurance-text asset; connected GitHub searches for `CIV-029` returned no matching PR/issue. Its detailed wording, applicable signatory variants and legal acceptance must therefore remain a named dependency, using the Hazine ve Maliye Bakanlığı / Kamu İç Kontrol Yönetmeliği EK-1/2/3 basis supplied in this task.

This was a **source audit**. No application server, database session, migration, test suite, browser workflow or exporter was run. No live database was contacted. A Python standard-library AST inspection of `main.py` and all `*routes.py` files confirmed the registered AR and export route inventory without importing the application. Existing tests were read, not rerun; their assertions are coverage evidence, not new passing-test results. Only this Markdown file is added; code, migrations, tests, bundles and workflow flags are unchanged.

CIV-014 did not mark its whole A1–A11 lines “Works”: it recorded Partial, Missing or Blocked verdicts. Working subfeatures must not be described as a completed statutory report.
