## 2. Proposed state machine, roles, reasons and audit

Use a separate `PPCorrection` lifecycle and isolated department workspace. **Do not turn an effective approved `Submission` into an editable `returned` submission.** Its approved content remains the effective baseline until a corrected publication commits. Every screen distinguishes “Yürürlükteki onaylı paket” from “Düzeltme çalışma kopyası”. Normal edit/submit/decision routes remain closed for that approved baseline; dedicated correction routes handle the workspace.

Eligibility: own organization, explicit period and approved department submission; period must be `active` or `draft`, department accessible and included, and no active correction for that department/period. An existing publication is optional: an approved but unpublished PP may be corrected and then published as version 1. A closed source period is read-only in this first implementation; do not reactivate it implicitly (owner question 4).

“Strateji” below distinguishes active Strategy staff from the active **Strateji Geliştirme Müdürü**. “Personel” and “Müdür” are own-department users; the latter is a submitter, not the final Strategy approver. Recheck active account, department, position and submission authority after lock waits; budget delegations confer no general correction authority.

| From → to | Action / proposed Turkish label | Authorized actor | Mandatory reason/evidence and effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approved baseline → `requested` | “Gerekçeli düzeltme iste” | Strateji staff or Müdür | Describe error, source/locator and expected correction; capture approved revision, full baseline snapshot/hash, scope/field allowlist, latest publication ID/hash if any and dependency digest. Notify department; no edit unlocked yet. |
| `requested` → `editing` | “Düzeltmeye başla” | Department Personel or Müdür | Acknowledge request with a nonblank note. Create first workspace revision from the frozen baseline; record editor and reason. |
| `editing` → `editing` | “Düzeltmeyi kaydet” | Department Personel or Müdür | Each actual change needs a change note, exact typed before/after diff and source evidence for value corrections. Append a workspace revision; never update an earlier revision. No-op saves create no edit event. |
| `editing` → `submitted` | “Düzeltmeyi incelemeye gönder” | Department Müdür or Personel with current `can_submit` | Summary of changes and response to each requested item; whole-package snapshot validation must pass and substantive diff must be nonempty. Freeze submitted workspace revision/hash and submitter authority; notify Strategy. |
| `submitted` → `returned` | “Düzeltmeyi gerekçeyle iade et” | Strateji Geliştirme Müdürü | Nonblank deficiencies tied to records/fields; submitted snapshot remains immutable. Notify department. |
| `returned` → `editing` | “İade edilen düzeltmeye devam et” | Department Personel or Müdür | Nonblank acknowledgement; derive a new workspace revision from the returned attempt. Prior submit/decision remains visible. Scope cannot expand. |
| `submitted` → `approved` | “Düzeltmeyi onayla” | Independent Strateji Geliştirme Müdürü | Nonblank approval reason; revalidate frozen package, dependencies and exact submitted hash. Store approval event/hash; workspace locks. Label: “Düzeltme onaylandı; yeni yayın bekleniyor.” Effective PP remains the old approved package. |
| Any nonterminal state, including `approved` → `cancelled` | “Düzeltme isteğini iptal et” | Strateji Geliştirme Müdürü | Nonblank cancellation reason; retain all requests, edits and decisions. Notify department/Strategy; effective data unchanged. Department may ask for cancellation in a note but cannot silently dismiss the request. |
| `approved` → `published` | “Yeni düzeltme sürümünü yayımla” | Independent Strateji Geliştirme Müdürü | Reason, exact included correction/approval IDs, expected predecessor and candidate hash, and current checklist evidence. Atomically publish and install approved corrections as described in section 4. |

`published` and `cancelled` are terminal. Editing an approved correction, widening its scope or reversing a published correction requires a new reasoned request. No direct approved→editing shortcut, automatic approval, timeout approval or client-supplied actor/time.

**Independence:** reuse the existing PP independent-approver check as a minimum and additionally reject any approver/publisher who edited any workspace revision in this correction cycle, even before an intermediate return or under a previous account position. Check actor IDs, not names or current roles. Capture authorship on every edit atomically; missing historical scope fails closed. Requesting, reading or returning a correction alone is not data authorship. A requester may approve only if all independence checks pass. No self-promotion or delegation exception; unresolved authority requires an audited account appointment through the existing administration flow.

Every successful command atomically persists the state projection, immutable event, revision/diff where applicable, `AuditLog` and required in-app notifications. Failure in any component rolls back everything; retries with the same idempotency key return the original result, without duplicate notices. No external messaging is introduced.

Every event requires: correction/organization/department/period IDs; sequence and from/to state; actor ID and name/role/position/authority snapshot; server UTC timestamp (display TSİ); reason; correlation/idempotency IDs; expected/actual lock version; baseline approval event/revision and digest; predecessor publication ID/hash if any; before/after workspace revision IDs and hashes; affected record IDs, field paths and typed diffs; relevant validation/evidence references. Submit, return, approval and publication events reference the exact attempt they decide. Keep null, absent value and numeric zero distinct in diffs. Store decimals as canonical strings; display numbers in Turkish format.

Suggested HTTP boundary: `POST /api/pp-corrections` then `POST /api/pp-corrections/{id}/{start|save|submit|return|approve|cancel}` and a period-level corrective publication command. Reads expose only authorized correction history/workspaces. All mutations require session/CSRF, explicit `period_id`, expected projection version and idempotency key; submit/approve also require the expected workspace hash. Invalid transition/stale data returns 409, permission failure 403, missing reason/invalid fields 422. Example: “Düzeltme #24 başka bir işlemde değişti. Kaydınız uygulanmadı; sayfayı yenileyip güncel değişiklikleri inceleyin.”

## 3. Proposed locks, edit scope and department isolation

The request freezes a **complete** department package for validation but grants a **specific field allowlist** for editing. Permission is checked from the actual diff on the server, never only from disabled controls or a posted record ID.

| Requested scope | What can change in the correction workspace | What stays locked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single indicator | Selected indicator name/unit/explanation/calculation/data-source fields and explicitly listed year/kind value cells. A cell may become blank or zero only as an explicit, reasoned change. | Indicator identity, owner/period, card/subprogram links, unrelated cells and all other records. |
| Single card | Listed card text fields, plus listed fields/cells of indicators already attached to that card. Request preview enumerates the exact children; new children do not silently acquire permission. | Other cards, membership/assignment, shared definitions, financial amounts and unlisted fields. Changing inherited card text lists all affected indicator definitions for reapproval. |
| Whole department package | Explicitly enumerated non-budget texts/indicator cells across that department, including activity descriptions. “Whole package” means broader selection, not unrestricted write access. | Monetary data, personnel/SGK, contract flag, economic/funding cells, catalogue/responsibility/scope, shared program/subprogram definitions, stable IDs and ownership remain locked. |

No record creation/deletion/reparenting in this first slice. Correct existing cards/text/indicator values without invoking draft-removal endpoints. Draft removal continues to enforce its own safeguards; a correction does not erase approved/publication evidence. General `ReportSection` book text and shared catalogue/program changes are outside department scope and require a later institution-level design (question 1). Denials explain the affected field and next step, for example: “Düzeltme #24 yalnız 17 numaralı göstergenin 2027 hedefini kapsıyor. Diğer alanları kaydedemezsiniz; kapsam için Strateji biriminden yeni düzeltme isteği talep edin.”

Baseline live rows and published snapshots stay locked throughout. Only `editing` grants workspace writes; `requested`, `submitted`, `returned`, `approved` and terminal states are read-only until an allowed transition. Dedicated screens show baseline, previous attempt and proposed values side by side, request/return reasons, who changed each field, and a persistent “Çalışma kopyası; yayımlanmış sürüm değişmedi.” notice. Form errors preserve input and focus the affected field. Strategy can inspect but cannot edit department content through this flow.

Other departments retain their own statuses, revisions, permissions and content. Department A's request does not revoke department B's approval or block B's independent draft work. Do not change global period state to unlock A. Different departments may have concurrent workspaces; only publication/version allocation is shared. A request blocks budget apply/reverse and contract-flag writes touching its department until cancellation/publication, including preview tokens issued before the request. Unrelated departments' budget operations remain available subject to publication freshness checks.

**Transaction locks, proposed common order:** organization → affected departments in ascending ID → periods in ascending ID → submissions and correction projections in ascending ID → content rows/indicator identities in deterministic order. Read/parse bodies before locks, execute synchronous locked work in a worker thread, reread all authority/state/digests after waiting, and use compare-and-swap projection versions. Hold no locks between browser requests. Organization locks serialize brief shared operations, not the human editing interval.

Integration must align ordinary publication with this order, coordinate period copy/activation and structural/import writes with correction reservations, and move the contract-flag path to acquire the shared department boundary before its activity lock. Approved-baseline content/scope/shared dependencies cannot change while reserved; an attempted shared definition/import/structure change touching a reserved department is rejected with its correction ID. Do not acquire an import batch lock after an organization lock: import apply/revert must keep their existing batch-first entry order and check reservations after obtaining the organization lock (`app/import_service.py:581–596`). Existing ordinary submit/review can retain their department-first subset provided they never acquire organization later. Publication must lock all relevant departments before its period lock. Test the combined orders, not only two correction requests.

Verified import integration anchor: `_lock_import_context` locks batch then organization, and both apply and revert call it (`app/import_service.py:581–600`, `app/import_service.py:656–657`). Adding correction reservations is an integration requirement; existing writers do not yet have those proposed checks.

## 4. Proposed publication, supersession and hash contract

1. Build a read-only publication candidate from effective approved department content overlaid with **all** approved corrections for the period. Reject if any nonterminal request in that period is not yet approved; ordinary publication must use the same gate so it cannot publish around an outstanding correction. Freeze exact correction revisions, approval events, department approval provenance, shared content/dependency hashes and expected latest publication. Departments without corrections must pass their existing gates. No partial inclusion of one correction attempt.
2. Compare with the previous publication and show every change. In the normal case, unchanged departments have equal semantic content. A budget operation or shared-book edit since the previous publication is an explicit additional difference and must be disclosed and evidenced; never silently restore old data or claim that a new book changed only the correction. Recheck the whole current book against the reviewed candidate digest. An unexpected difference aborts; no automatic merge/rebase.
3. Extend the shared publication gates with correction completeness, same-attempt independent approval and reservation/freshness checks. For **corrective publication**, require fresh evidence for every mandatory automatic/manual release-checklist item and named owner sign-off. This is a proposed enforcement addition; section 1 documents that the current publish route does not enforce manual items. Bind confirmations to the final overlaid candidate, correction event sequence, accounts/schema/app context and expected predecessor. An additional edit, return, cancellation, budget event or new publication invalidates that context.
4. On publish, take common locks, reread all baselines, correction approvals and checklist evidence, verify predecessor is still latest, then apply the approved allowlisted diffs to live rows and validate the resulting full package/book. Advance each affected `Submission.revision` by one and keep `status=approved`; append a `correction_applied` submission event linking the real correction submit/approval events and full new snapshot. Never manufacture an ordinary `approve` event by the publisher. Enhance approval-evidence resolution to accept this explicit, hash-bound provenance; ordinary `approve` evidence still works unchanged. No live installation occurs at correction approval time.
5. Freeze indicator definitions and bindings through the existing definition contract. Preserve identity; create a definition revision if changed effective text/target needs one, reuse matching definitions otherwise. Canonicalize the final full snapshot, insert `Publication.version=max+1` under the period lock and existing unique constraint, insert immutable correction/publication provenance and supersession relationship, append published/audit/notification events and mark requests `published`, in **one transaction**. A tightly scoped installation service must verify request scope/hash/approval and record human editors; do not expose `controlled_write` as an unrestricted bypass or count the technical installer as the content author. Re-run validation after installation before commit. Rollback restores every live row, submission revision and event if freeze, binding, lineage, evidence or notification persistence fails.
6. With no previous publication, this creates version 1 and no supersession edge. Otherwise append one edge from the immediately preceding publication to the new publication. Requests opened against an older publication fail freshness and must be cancelled/recreated after reviewed comparison; never retarget them silently. Duplicate publication retries return the same publication ID. A lost race to another version returns 409 before any durable change.

**Old version visibility:** never update/delete an old `Publication`, snapshot, hash, definition, binding or export. Store supersession in a separate append-only relationship; derive list labels from it. Show “Sürüm 3 — Önceki sürüm; Sürüm 4 ile güncellendi.” and “Sürüm 4 — Güncel sürüm; düzeltme #24.” with date, authorized actor, reason summary and links to both versions/history. Existing direct Word/PDF/JSON links keep working with their original content. Keep Strategy visibility unless a separate access-policy task expands it; do not add public access here. The verified export path hashes JSON source bytes and renders Word/PDF from that JSON (`app/program_routes.py:430–435`); it does not hash or archive the generated file bytes. Preserve old JSON byte-for-byte and its rendered content; byte-identical historical Word/PDF files across future renderer changes would require separate immutable artifact storage. Never inject a later supersession banner into an old export.

**Hash contract:** SHA-256 of exact stored canonical UTF-8 JSON (`ensure_ascii=False`, sorted object keys, compact separators), with deterministic collection order (records by stable ID/position, value cells by year/kind, links and manifest items by stable key) and decimal strings. Use the same ordering for request/workspace/candidate digests; freeze the serialization contract rather than depending on ORM relationship iteration order. Include the full book data (all departments, cards, indicator values/text, activities/costs, economics/aggregates, catalogue, sections/images) and existing definition-contract markers/identity/revision references. Add a versioned `pp_correction_manifest` inside new snapshots: predecessor ID/version/hash or null; sorted correction IDs; submitted workspace and approval-event IDs/hashes; affected department baseline/final hashes; scope/diff digests; submitting/approving actors and times; release candidate/evidence digest and confirmation IDs. Avoid a self-hash cycle: the manifest contains the prepublication candidate digest, not this publication's final hash. Hash all these manifest fields too. Supersession edges reference the final stored hash but are outside the old snapshot; later edges cannot alter it. Operational notes, read flags and live “current” labels are outside the content hash. Old snapshots retain their original bytes/schema and hash rules.

Candidate hashing has its own named contract: canonical overlaid book plus frozen corrections/approvals, predecessor, accounts/schema/app context and validation results, **excluding** confirmation IDs, publication-generated IDs/time and the candidate's own digest. Confirmation rows bind that digest. The final manifest records their IDs after confirmation. Subsequent readiness may show a new context after publication because publication history changed; retain the exact prepublication checklist evidence used for the published candidate rather than rewriting confirmations.

Activity Reports already referencing an older PP publication/definition keep those references. A corrected PP publication does not recalculate approved actuals or move a ReportCandidate/ReportRelease to a new target. Surface that downstream impact for Strategy review; a later Activity Report correction uses its own guarded workflow.

## 5. Proposed data model, one migration and rollback

Use new PP-specific models; do not overload Activity Report revision requests or add a mutable `superseded` field to `publications`.

| New table / model | Required fields and constraints |
| --- | --- |
| `pp_corrections` / `PPCorrection` | Immutable request: ID, organization/department/period/submission FKs; reason and source evidence; scope kind and canonical allowlist; baseline submission revision and nullable approval-event FK (or explicitly identified legacy evidence), full baseline and validation-context JSON/hash; dependency hash; optional predecessor publication FK/hash; requester/actor snapshot/time; request idempotency key. Match department/period/organization across references with DB validation. No cascade deletion. |
| `pp_correction_states` | One projection per request: state, `lock_version`, latest workspace revision ID, submitted revision ID, approved event ID, last transition-event ID and published publication ID where applicable. CHECK states and valid pointer combinations; guarded compare-and-swap updates and no DELETE. Immutable request scope/baseline cannot be changed through projection writes. |
| `pp_correction_reservations` | Active request ID plus organization/department/period and status. Unique `(department_id, period_id)` while active (PostgreSQL partial unique index); one reservation per request. Release by audited transition to inactive in the same transaction as cancellation/publication. Add DB guards requiring the corresponding event and matching projection. No deletion, and no reactivation of terminal requests. This enforces exclusivity without indexing state in another table. |
| `pp_correction_revisions` | Request FK, monotonic sequence, previous revision FK, complete canonical department workspace plus frozen validation context/hash, canonical typed diff, reason/evidence, actor/time. Unique request/sequence; predecessor and request must match. Append only, including edit attempts that are later returned or cancelled. |
| `pp_correction_events` | All section 2 audit fields, frozen authority snapshot, attempt/hash/validation references and per-request sequence; unique request/sequence and request/idempotency key. Append only. Deferred PostgreSQL constraint triggers validate at commit that projection/reservation changes and their referenced transition event agree; they also validate ownership/revision references and approval independence from revision authors. Service checks provide the corresponding SQLite behavior; PostgreSQL is the enforcement/locking acceptance target. Separate publication command idempotency covers a batch of corrections. |
| `pp_correction_publications` | Immutable publication command/provenance: publication FK unique, period, candidate digest, expected predecessor, command idempotency key unique per period, reason, publisher/time, checklist evidence manifest. Join table `pp_correction_publication_items` uniquely consumes each correction once and references exact approved event/revision. Cross-check request, department, period and final hashes. |
| `pp_publication_successions` | Predecessor publication FK unique, successor publication FK unique, publication-command FK, both hashes, reason, actor/time. Enforce distinct IDs, same period and successor version greater than predecessor; successor must have been latest predecessor's next version under the lock. Append only; no forks or cycles. |

Store full private reasons/source locators in authorized history. The publication manifest includes traceable event/diff digests and necessary approval metadata, not unnecessary personal/contact data or arbitrary confidential source text. New tables are append only except guarded state/reservation projections; ORM and PostgreSQL UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE guards protect history. Extend role audits to include new tables/triggers and prevent app DDL, role escalation and guard disabling. Do not add SECURITY DEFINER helpers or grant runtime ownership. Scope checks in services remain mandatory; append-only storage alone does not authorize an insert.

**Exactly one future migration:** proposed `c070a1b2c3d4_pp_post_approval_corrections.py`, `revision='c070a1b2c3d4'`, `down_revision='c067a1b2c3d4'`, no branch labels. At this baseline the single Alembic graph head is `c067a1b2c3d4`; its declared predecessor is `c063a1b2c3d4` (`migrations/versions/c067a1b2c3d4_release_confirmations.py:5–8`). Recompute the real head before implementation and chain this single new migration after it if development has advanced; never edit an existing migration or create a competing head.

Upgrade outline: create request/revision/event/projection/reservation/provenance/lineage tables in FK-safe order (defer circular projection pointers until creation); add indexes and checks; install immutable history and projection-transition guards, PostgreSQL TRUNCATE guards and SQLite-compatible history guards; grant only needed runtime access using the existing owner/runtime arrangement. No publication rewrite, hash recomputation, identity reassignment or fabricated historical approval. No backfilled corrections. If an approved legacy baseline has no trustworthy approval event, require reviewed historical evidence captured with actor/date/source and its baseline hash before opening a correction; flag it as legacy evidence rather than inventing an ordinary approval. Run owner migration and restricted-runtime smoke tests on a populated disposable database.

**Rollback:** before any correction exists, downgrade may remove only the new empty objects in reverse FK order. If any request, revision, event, reservation, provenance or succession exists, downgrade fails before DDL; preserve all evidence and one head. Operational rollback disables new correction commands and retains read-only history and all publications. Resolve pending work by audited cancellation before returning to a release that cannot interpret correction states; never run older writing code against unexplained active reservations. After publication, undo content through a new forward correction and new publication, not DELETE, hash replacement or restoring an old database over later history. A populated schema rollback needs a separately reviewed, lossless compatibility migration. This specification creates no migration.

## 7. Proposed risks and edge cases

| Risk / edge | Design decision and recovery |
| --- | --- |
| Correction during an open period copy | Copy and correction publication share the organization lock. Copy reads only effective approved data, never an editing/submitted workspace. Record copy provenance (source period, source latest publication/hash if any, relevant baseline hashes and pending correction IDs) in the existing copy audit. If copy wins, later publication creates a visible follow-up discrepancy warning for the new period; if publication wins, copy sees the corrected structure. Do not silently propagate values or edits into the target period. Target department decides through its own draft/correction flow, preserving distinct year cells and persistent identity. |
| Activation closes a source period mid-cycle | Activation must reject closing a period with outstanding corrections and name them. Owner must finish or cancel those requests first. If an out-of-band close is observed, block subsequent commands and retain workspace/history; only audited cancellation is permitted. No implicit reactivation or global unlock. |
| Concurrent corrections | One active reservation per department/period, even for disjoint indicators; separate departments may proceed independently. Scope widening uses cancellation plus new request. All period requests must be approved or cancelled before a new corrective publication. Stale predecessor/candidate hash requires explicit comparison and new request/confirmations, not last-write-wins. |
| Budget reallocation/reversal | Remains a separate legal workflow with protected monetary lines. Block operations whose legs touch reserved department/period pairs; reject old signed previews after reservation creation, including a request later cancelled (include reservation event sequence in freshness so create/cancel is detectable). If budget apply wins first, the correction captures its resulting revision/hash; existing candidates become stale. Monetary errors are routed to budget/legal review, not smuggled through a whole-department scope. A reversal after correction publication retains its existing later-line checks and must appear in any later book comparison. |
| Contract flag and shared/import writers | Flag changes have a separate activity-first exception today (`app/budget_service.py:527–535`); add reservation checks and compatible locking there. Import uses batch→organization serialization (`app/import_service.py:581–600`, `app/import_service.py:656–657`); preserve that entry order. Shared program/catalogue, mappings, source verification, economic split, organization structure and scope writers need reservation checks and digest invalidation. Service callers must not evade checks by omitting a request-session flag. |
| Already approved workspace becomes invalid | Changed dependency/permission/validation digest blocks publication and leaves live data untouched. Cancel and open a new scoped correction for further edits; all previous approval/evidence stays visible. Do not auto-return an approved snapshot or reuse its approval for a different hash. |
| Cancellation, outage and bad publication | Before publication, cancellation retains workspace attempts and restores no data because live content never changed. Transient transaction failure leaves no partial installation/version/edge/notice. After publication, repair with a forward correction producing another version. Retried commands use server-stored idempotency results. |
| Missing legacy approval/history | Fail closed unless baseline evidence can be traced and explicitly recorded. Label legacy evidence distinctly. If any PP `DirectoratePackage` exists for the same department/period, reject this legacy correction entry and direct Strategy to review that package workflow; do not mutate its immutable package revisions/projections from the new PP service. |
| Checklist and downstream consumers | Candidate-aware confirmations are required before publication; the existing live-data digest cannot alone describe workspace content. Keep old confirmation history and do not infer municipal acceptance from tests. Old Activity Report targets remain unchanged; alert Strategy if the correction changes an inherited definition/target used downstream. |
| Scope/size and disclosure | Card text may alter multiple inherited definitions; enumerate them before submission. Department-wide scope still forbids money/deletion/shared data. Set bounded request/snapshot sizes and indexed period/request history reads; reject oversized submissions with a Turkish recovery instruction. Avoid exposing confidential reasons/source documents in downloadable publication metadata. |

Preserve the verified batch-first import order and shared organization boundary from #66 (`app/import_service.py:581–596`); never call import apply/revert from inside a correction transaction that already holds the organization lock.

## 8. İş sahibine açık sorular (önerilen yanıtlarla)

1. **“Metin düzeltmesi” kurumun genel kitap bölümlerini de kapsamalı mı?** Önerim: İlk uygulama müdürlük kartı/gösterge/faaliyet metinleriyle sınırlı olsun. Genel kitap bölümleri ve ortak katalog için kurum düzeyinde, ayrı onay sorumlusu olan bir tasarım hazırlansın; müdürlük kapsamı üzerinden ortak veri açılmasın.
2. **Bir müdürlükte aynı anda birden fazla düzeltme açılsın mı?** Önerim: Hayır. Dönem ve müdürlük başına tek açık istek olsun; farklı müdürlükler eşzamanlı çalışabilsin. Yeni yayın için dönemdeki tüm açık istekler onaylansın veya gerekçeyle iptal edilsin.
3. **Müdürün gönderim yetkisi verdiği Personel düzeltmeyi yeniden gönderebilsin mi?** Önerim: Evet, mevcut gönderim kuralı korunsun. Strateji Personeli istek açabilsin; iade, onay ve düzeltme yayını yalnız bağımsız Strateji Geliştirme Müdürü tarafından yapılsın. Onay gerekçesi zorunlu olsun.
4. **Kapalı dönemde de düzeltme yapılması ilk uygulamaya dahil mi?** Önerim: İlk uygulamada kapalı dönem salt okunur kalsın. Kapalı yıl zorunluysa genel dönemi yeniden açmadan, süreli ve gerekçeli özel düzeltme penceresi ile yayın kontrol listesi politikasını ayrıca kararlaştıralım; mevcut tasarıma açıkça eklemeden uygulamaya geçmeyelim.
5. **Düzeltme yayını için tüm güncel kontrol listesi teyitleri ve iş sahibi onayı yeniden istensin mi?** Önerim: Evet. Teyitler yeni adayın özetiyle ilişkilendirilsin; önceki yayınların teyitleri geçmiş olarak korunsun. Hatalı yayımlanmış bir düzeltme de silinmeden, yeni gerekçe/onay ve daha yeni sürümle düzeltilebilsin.
