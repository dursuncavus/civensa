GOAL
Directorates enter 2026 actuals and build the 2026 Faaliyet Raporu from a PP publication: ActivityReport revisions are created from a Publication (app/activity_contract_service.py ~line 372, publication_bindings). The 2026 period will hold the approved, printed 2026 Performans Programı, loaded unchanged by the first-load command (CIV-165, approved IDML sha256 1aaa0bb8…). Its publication is blocked: publication_checks() (app/program_service.py:539) are authoring gates (department scope, validate_department, Strateji approval per directorate, catalogue, cost reconciliation) and they fail for a book that was approved outside Civensa (CIV-169 K1: 174 of 182 cost activities not linked to the catalogue, plus other check errors). The same will happen for 2027 (also first-loaded from its approved book, D-327). Build a guarded "Onaylı kitaptan yayınla" path so Strategy can create the publication of a first-loaded approved book, without weakening the normal authoring path.
