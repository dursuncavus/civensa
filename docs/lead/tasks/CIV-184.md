CIV-184 Meclis sunumu fotoğrafları: yükleme, saklama, fotoğraf ve önce/sonra slaytları

Follow AGENTS.md. Base branch: feature/authorization-workflow-phase5. Never merge, never deploy, never connect to the live database.

GOAL
Strateji users upload photos of completed works for a Faaliyet Raporu year; the photos are resized and stored in PostgreSQL; Strateji selects and orders them; the İdare council deck (CIV-182/183 recipe) shows them as full-screen photo slides and "önce / sonra" comparison slides, looking and behaving exactly as the lead's design reference.

RULES
1. Model (one Alembic migration): table sunum_photos — id, organisation/period scoping consistent with ar_documents, report year, department_id (the müdürlük the work belongs to), caption (required, ≤ 140 chars), taken_on (date, optional), mahalle (optional, ≤ 80), kind ('single' | 'before' | 'after'), pair_key (links a before with its after), webp_data (LargeBinary), thumb_data (LargeBinary), width, height, sha256, byte_size, selected (bool), sort_order (int), created_by, created_at. Constraints: a pair has exactly one before and one after from the same department/year; sha256 unique per year (duplicate upload → Turkish error).
2. Upload: JPEG, PNG or WebP only, ≤ 15 MB raw (read_upload_limited). Verify the real format with Pillow (not the extension). Apply EXIF orientation, then STRIP all metadata (EXIF/GPS). Resize so the long edge is 1600 px (never upscale), encode WebP quality 82; thumbnail long edge 320 px. Store only the processed images, never the original. Reject decompression bombs (Pillow MAX_IMAGE_PIXELS) with a Turkish message. HEIC → Turkish message "iPhone fotoğrafını JPEG olarak kaydedip yükleyin." Required checkbox before upload: "Fotoğrafta tanınabilir kişi yoksa ya da kişilerin izni alındıysa işaretleyin." (store the confirmation with the row).
3. Screen (Strateji only, require_strategy): under the Faaliyet Raporu area, page "Meclis sunumu fotoğrafları" for a year: upload form (müdürlük, tür: tek / önce-sonra çifti, açıklama, tarih, mahalle), grid of thumbnails per müdürlük, toggle "Sunumda göster", up/down order, delete (with confirmation; deletes the row — these are not report records). Use the existing Civensa layout, components and Turkish texts style; no new design system.
4. Deck: the İdare recipe adds a "Tamamlanan işler" group after the Bütçe group with the selected photos in sort_order: 'single' → photo slide; a complete before/after pair → önce/sonra slide. Caption band shows caption, mahalle, müdürlük, date (Turkish date format; missing parts omitted, no "—" in the band). An incomplete pair or no selected photo → no slide and a log line. Photos embedded as data URIs (no http(s) reference). #pdf: photo slide as one page; önce/sonra side by side, no divider handle.
5. Size: raise the single-file limit only for the İdare council deck to 8 MB; cap selected photos at 16 (screen refuses the 17th with a Turkish message). Birim decks keep 3 MB and stay byte-identical when no photos exist.
6. Photos are NOT part of the approved report payload and do not change the verification code; the deck footer source for photo slides is "Fotoğraf: <müdürlük>".
