WORK
1. Shared helper (one place, used by both readers): a story counts as printed when at least one of its text frames (including threaded frames and frames anchored inside other printed stories) intersects a page rectangle of its spread. Tables in non-printed stories are excluded from loading and from all checks, and listed in the dry-run report in a short section "Basılmayan (pasteboard) tablolar — yüklenmedi" with story id, spread pages and the table's first header cells. Never exclude silently; never exclude a table whose frame overlaps a page even partly.
2. Unit tests with a tiny synthetic IDML (or a minimal in-memory zip): on-page table kept, pasteboard-only table excluded and reported, partly-overlapping frame kept, threaded story with one on-page frame kept, anchored frame inside a printed story kept.
3. Run the dry-run on the approved IDML exactly as documented in dryrun-printed.md (disposable local PostgreSQL) → new `docs/first-load/dryrun-onayli.md`. Do not edit dryrun-printed.md or dryrun-d3.md.

Then counts of book-check items / ready departments old vs new, and which old book-check items (41) are now resolved, still open, or new. Blank, dash and zero stay distinct.
