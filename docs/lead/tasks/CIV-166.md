CIV-166 Mali Hizmetler verisi birim gönderimini kilitlemesin

Follow AGENTS.md. Base branch: feature/authorization-workflow-phase5. Never merge, never deploy, never connect to the live database.

GOAL
Strateji (Dr. Ali Kaya, e-mail 2 Nov 2026, D-361 ML2/ML3) says budget figures belong to Mali Hizmetler and Mali Hizmetler's figures are authoritative. Today a directorate cannot submit its unit Faaliyet Raporu while Mali Hizmetler's budget data for that directorate is missing or inconsistent, although the directorate cannot edit that data. Make those Mali Hizmetler-owned items visible warnings in the directorate (unit) report, not submission blockers. The İdare report keeps blocking as today.

CURRENT STATE (DOĞRULANDI by the lead on phase5 aab72de)
1. app/activity_financial_service.py financial_data(db, doc): for a unit document (doc.department_id set) it reads Mali Hizmetler's budget rows (source = idare_document) filtered to that directorate, and appends to result['missing']['III.A.1']:
   a. blank-field lines ("…: … boş; bilinmiyorsa boş kalır, sıfırsa 0 girin."),
   b. missing-rows lines ("…: Ekonomik kod/Program … gider satırları eksik. Mali Hizmetler ilgili müdürlüğün tutarlarını girmeli …"),
   c. empty-table lines ("… gider tablosu boş. Mali Hizmetler verileri geldiğinde …"),
   d. result['checks'] (Yıl Sonu Ödeneği tutarsız, negative year-end allowance, economic-code total ≠ programme total), merged into missing by `result['missing']['III.A.1'] += result['checks']`,
   e. deviation-explanation lines ("Sapma açıklaması gerekli: …") — row explanation may come from Mali Hizmetler ('explanation') or the directorate ('birim_explanation').
2. app/activity_sections_service.py report_data() adds result['financial']['missing'][code] to each section's row['missing'].
3. app/ar_unit_workflow_service.py completeness() treats any non-empty section 'missing' as incomplete; transition 'submit' raises 409 "Birim faaliyet raporu gönderilemedi. Şu bölümleri tamamlayın: …". So a-d block the directorate.
4. The directorate can only write its own explanation (birim_explanation, 'explanations' entries); budget amounts are Mali Hizmetler's (activity_financial_service.authorize, line ~66).
5. app/templates/activity_financial.html prints all missing items as `.ar-warning` paragraphs under "Malî Bilgiler · Eksikler ve Kontroller".
